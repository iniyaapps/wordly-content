Privacy Policy

Wordly · last updated 19 September 2026

The short version

Wordly has no accounts and no servers of its own. Everything you do in the game — your progress, statistics, streak, ratings and settings — is stored only on your phone and is never sent to us. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects data for that purpose under its own policy.

Information we collect

We collect no personal information. Wordly never asks for your name, email address, phone number, postal address, date of birth or any government identifier, and there is no account to create.

You may optionally choose a nickname and an avatar emoji. Both are stored on your device only.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following in a local database on your phone:

  • a randomly generated identifier used only to link rows in that local database
  • your optional nickname and avatar
  • levels played and completed, scores, hints used and completion times
  • statistics, your daily streak and unlocked achievements
  • puzzle ratings you choose to give, and words you mark as favourites
  • your settings, and the language packs you have downloaded

None of this is uploaded. It is also excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so it does not leave your phone even that way. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Downloading word content

Puzzle content is downloaded from a public GitHub Pages website. These are ordinary requests for static files: no account, no cookies, and nothing about you is transmitted beyond what any web request necessarily includes (such as your IP address, which GitHub may log as the host of that site).

Advertising

Wordly is free and is funded by advertising through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use device identifiers and similar information. That processing is governed by Google's own privacy policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where the law requires your consent — including the EEA, the UK and Switzerland — the app shows Google's consent form before requesting any ad, and no ad is requested until you have made a choice. You can change that choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options.

Rewarded ads are always optional. You decide whether to watch one, and the reward is granted only once the ad has actually finished.

Analytics

Wordly includes no analytics SDK. We do not track how you use the app. The statistics you see in the app are calculated on your device from your own play, for you.

Children

Wordly is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app requests family-safe ad content from Google. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help.

Your choices

  • Delete your data: Settings → Reset all progress permanently deletes your levels, statistics, streak, ratings and achievements from the device. Uninstalling removes everything.
  • Advertising consent: Settings → Ad privacy options, where your region offers it.
  • Advertising ID: Android's own settings let you reset or delete it, which limits ad personalisation across all apps.

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on your behalf — your device holds it all.
